**4.** Image: `images/03-draft.jpg` · 174 characters

> Open an email, press Draft with AI, and the reply writes itself into the box. Edit it, redraft it, or send it.
>

**5.** Image: `images/06-ai-settings.jpg` · 207 characters

> The AI runs on the plan you already pay for.
> 
> Mail finds Claude Code or Codex on your Mac, uses the lightest model by default (Haiku, or Luna on Codex), and shows how much of your limit is left. No API keys.

**6.** Image: `images/08-privacy.jpg` · 212 characters

> No Crooli account. No Crooli servers.
> 
> Your mail goes between your Mac and your provider. Passwords sit in the macOS Keychain. Remote images are off by default, so most senders cannot tell you opened their email.

**7.** Image: `images/07-install.jpg` · 213 characters

> Install it with one line in Terminal:
> 
> curl -fsSL https://mail.crooli.com/install.sh | bash
> 
> macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Because it is fetched with curl, macOS shows no "damaged app" warning. Windows is next.
